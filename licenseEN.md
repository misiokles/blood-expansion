# Sprite / Model License and Attribution

This file records the origin of the sprites and models used by Blood Expansion and indicates which assets are used in full and which were modified or recolored for the mod.

## Asset origins

- **Blood Wraith** — **Necropolis Upgrades Mod** — used in full.
- **Scarlet Birds** — **Third Upgrades Mod** — used in full.
- **Crimson Dragon** — **Third Upgrades Mod** — used in full.
- **Blood Skeleton** — **Third Upgrades Mod** — used in full.
- **Bloody Mistress** — **Necropolis Creatures Pack Mod** — used in full.
- **Blood Angel** — **Necropolis Creatures Pack Mod** — used in full.
- **Blood Elemental** — model taken from **Forgotten Fields Creatures Pack Mod**; the animation was slightly adjusted, and the recolor was created independently for Blood Expansion.
- **Blood Firebird** — independently created recolor.
- **Blood Phoenix** — independently created recolor.
- **Scarlet Wraith** — independently created recolor.

This file is an attribution and asset-origin notice. It does not replace or modify the licenses of the original mods and does not grant additional rights to their authors’ original materials. Original models and animations remain subject to the terms of their respective source mods.
